Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 19, 2026

The short version. Fit Check has no sign-up, no ads and no tracking across apps. The App gets a random anonymous ID so we can apply the free plan, unlock what you bought and see how the App is used. Your outfit photo is sent to our server and to an AI provider only to rate it, is processed in memory, and is never stored or used to train AI models by us. Your fit history stays on your iPhone.

1. Who we are

Fit Check (the "App") is published by Quentin Lauret, an individual developer based in France ("we", "us"). We are the data controller for the processing described here. Contact: quentin.lauret@epita.fr.

3. Service providers

We share data only with providers that process it for us, under contract:

  • Anthropic (AI analysis of the outfit photo, with its Claude models through its commercial API). Anthropic does not use data sent through its commercial API to train its models. It keeps it only for a limited time, longer only where needed to enforce its usage policies, then deletes it.
  • BytePlus (ModelArk image generation, "Upgrade my fit" only). Processes the photo and tips to produce the upgraded image.
  • Apple (App Store payments and subscriptions, under Apple's own privacy policy).
  • RevenueCat (manages subscriptions and purchases, shows the Fit Check+ offer, and measures its results). Receives your anonymous ID and purchase information from the App and from Apple.
  • Supabase (hosts our database and server functions: your anonymous ID, quotas, usage events, purchase events and result reports).

Before your first analysis, the App names these AI providers and asks for your consent to send your photo to them. Some providers are located outside the European Economic Area (for example in the United States or Singapore); in that case transfers rely on the European Commission's Standard Contractual Clauses or an adequacy decision.

We do not sell your data, share it for advertising, or use it to track you across apps or websites.

4. Legal bases (GDPR)

  • Performance of a contract: rating your outfit, Fit Check+ features, verifying purchases.
  • Consent: sending your photo to the AI provider. You can withdraw it at any time by not submitting photos.
  • Legitimate interests: security, abuse prevention, review of reported results, and measuring how the App and the Fit Check+ offer are used.
  • Legal obligation: keeping purchase records where required.

5. Children

Fit Check is not intended for children under 13 and blocks them at first launch. We do not knowingly collect data from children under 13. If you believe a child has used the App, contact us and we will delete any related data we hold.

6. Your rights

You can ask to access, correct or delete your data, object to or restrict its processing, and receive a copy of it. Because there is no sign-up and we keep no photos, we hold nothing that names you; include your anonymous ID (in the App under You → Your anonymous ID) so we can find the records linked to it. Write to quentin.lauret@epita.fr; we answer within one month.

If you are in the EU, you can also file a complaint with your data protection authority; in France, the CNIL (cnil.fr). If you live in California or another US state with a privacy law, you have similar rights, and we do not sell or share personal information as defined by those laws.

7. Security

All traffic between the App and our server uses HTTPS. Photos are kept in memory only for the time of the request. Our database only accepts writes from the App for your own anonymous ID and lets the App read nothing back; access to it is restricted.

8. Changes

We will update this page if our practices change and change the date above. Significant changes will be announced in the App.
