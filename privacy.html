Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 17, 2026

The short version. Fit Check has no accounts, no ads, no analytics and no tracking. Your outfit photo is sent to our server and to an AI provider only to rate it, is processed in memory, and is never stored or used to train AI models by us. Your fit history stays on your iPhone.

1. Who we are

Fit Check (the "App") is published by Quentin Lauret, an individual developer based in France ("we", "us"). We are the data controller for the processing described here. Contact: quentin.lauret@epita.fr.

2. What we process, and why

DataWhyWhere it goes / how long
Outfit photo you take or choose, with the occasion and judge you pick To rate your outfit and write tips (the core feature) Sent over HTTPS to our server, with location (EXIF/GPS) metadata removed on your device and again on the server. Processed in memory, then discarded: never written to disk and never logged. Forwarded to our AI provider for the analysis (see section 3).
Outfit photo and the tips, when you use "Upgrade my fit" (Fit Check+) To generate an image of your outfit with the tips applied Same as above; forwarded to our image-generation provider (see section 3). The generated image is returned to your iPhone and not kept by us.
Fit history (photos, scores, tips) So you can look back at your fits Stored only on your iPhone. We never receive it. Delete a fit in the App, or delete the App to erase everything.
Result reports (the text of the result you report, the reason, your optional comment, a random result ID) To review flagged results and improve safety Stored on our server, without any photo, for up to 12 months.
Purchase information (Apple's signed transaction for Fit Check+ or an extra upgrade) To check your subscription, apply the daily upgrade quota and make sure each extra upgrade is used once Verified by our server. Daily quota counters are kept in memory and reset every day. For extra upgrades we keep the transaction ID, an anonymous subscriber ID from Apple, and the date it was used, for as long as needed for accounting and fraud prevention (up to 3 years). We never receive your name, Apple ID or payment details: Apple handles payments.
IP address Rate limiting and protecting the service from abuse Kept in memory for up to one hour. Our hosting provider may keep standard technical logs for a short period for security.
Age check (birthday or Apple's Declared Age Range) and on-device settings (e.g. date of your last free fit check) To keep the App 13+ and apply the free plan's daily limit Stays on your iPhone. Your birthday is not sent to us.

The AI analyzes the clothes in the photo only. It is instructed not to comment on your body, face or any personal characteristic, and we do not use photos to identify anyone.

3. Service providers

We share data only with providers that process it for us, under contract:

  • OpenAI (AI analysis of the outfit photo). Data sent through OpenAI's API is not used to train its models by default; OpenAI may keep it for a limited time (up to 30 days) to detect abuse, then deletes it.
  • BytePlus (ModelArk image generation, "Upgrade my fit" only). Processes the photo and tips to produce the upgraded image.
  • Apple (App Store payments and subscriptions, under Apple's own privacy policy).
  • Our hosting provider, which runs our server.

Before your first analysis, the App asks for your consent to send your photo to the AI provider. Some providers are located outside the European Economic Area (for example in the United States or Singapore); in that case transfers rely on the European Commission's Standard Contractual Clauses or an adequacy decision.

We do not sell your data, share it for advertising, or use it to track you across apps or websites.

4. Legal bases (GDPR)

  • Performance of a contract: rating your outfit, Fit Check+ features, verifying purchases.
  • Consent: sending your photo to the AI provider. You can withdraw it at any time by not submitting photos.
  • Legitimate interests: security, abuse prevention and review of reported results.
  • Legal obligation: keeping purchase records where required.

5. Children

Fit Check is not intended for children under 13 and blocks them at first launch. We do not knowingly collect data from children under 13. If you believe a child has used the App, contact us and we will delete any related data we hold.

6. Your rights

You can ask to access, correct or delete your data, object to or restrict its processing, and receive a copy of it. Because we have no accounts and keep no photos, we usually hold nothing that identifies you; include any result ID or transaction ID you have so we can find related records. Write to quentin.lauret@epita.fr; we answer within one month.

If you are in the EU, you can also file a complaint with your data protection authority; in France, the CNIL (cnil.fr). If you live in California or another US state with a privacy law, you have similar rights, and we do not sell or share personal information as defined by those laws.

7. Security

All traffic between the App and our server uses HTTPS. Photos are kept in memory only for the time of the request. Access to our server is restricted.

8. Changes

We will update this page if our practices change and change the date above. Significant changes will be announced in the App.
