Frequently asked questions

How does Fit Check work?

Take a full-body photo of your outfit (or choose one from your library), pick an occasion and a judge, and the AI gives your outfit a score out of 10 with three tips. You can then share a 9:16 card to your stories.

Does the AI judge my body or face?

No. The AI is instructed to rate the clothes only: colors, cuts, fit of the garments and accessories. If a result ever comments on you as a person, please tap "Report" on the result so we can review it.

Are my photos stored?

No. Your photo is sent securely to our server and to the AI provider, analyzed in memory, then discarded. Your fit history is stored only on your iPhone. See the Privacy Policy for details.

How do I get better results?
  • Show your full outfit, head to toe, in good light.
  • Use a plain background and stand straight in front of the camera.
  • Use the timer if no one can take the photo for you.
What does Fit Check+ include?

Unlimited fit checks (the free plan has one per day) and "Upgrade my fit", which shows your outfit redrawn with the tips applied, up to a daily limit shown in the App. After that limit, you can buy one extra upgrade.

How do I restore my purchase on a new iPhone?

Sign in with the same Apple ID, open Fit Check, go to Fit Check+ and tap "Restore purchases".

How do I cancel my subscription?
  1. Open the Settings app on your iPhone.
  2. Tap your name, then Subscriptions.
  3. Select Fit Check+ and tap Cancel Subscription.

You keep Fit Check+ until the end of the period you paid for. Deleting the App does not cancel the subscription.

How do I get a refund?

Payments are handled by Apple, so we can't issue refunds ourselves. Request one at reportaproblem.apple.com.

How do I delete my data?

Your fit history is stored on your iPhone: delete a fit from your history, or delete the App to erase everything. We don't keep your photos and you don't have an account with us. For any other request about your data, email us.

The analysis failed or takes too long.

Check your internet connection and try again. If your photo is refused, make sure it shows an outfit clearly. If it keeps happening, contact us with the time of the attempt.

I saw an inappropriate result. What should I do?

Tap "Report" on the result and choose a reason. We review every report. You can also email us.
